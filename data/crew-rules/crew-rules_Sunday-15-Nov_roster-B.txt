1. Every crew needs 1 team leader and 1 first aider whose certificate is valid on the day of the clean-up, Sunday 15 November 2026.
2. Volunteers must be 18 or older. Anyone whose age is not stated must be asked before being assigned.
3. Only a volunteer with a current electrician licence may check wiring, switchboards or flooded appliances, or turn power back on. Expired licences and students do not count.
4. If the photo or notes show possible structural damage (cracks, sagging or water-stained ceiling, leaning wall, rotten floorboards), the house is Not ready until checked. Do not send a crew.
5. Do not move, open or use gas cylinders. Report them to the team leader.
6. Maximum 5 volunteers per crew, including the team leader. Work in pairs.
7. Volunteers without their own transport may go to a house more than 1 km from Surau Contoh Baru only if a crew member's vehicle has a free seat. Free seats are listed in the roster.
8. Houses with bulky waste need a lorry or van. If none is free, list it as a gap.
9. The session runs from 8:00am to 12:00pm. Volunteers must be available for the whole session. Include a 10-minute water break every hour.
10. Priority: households with babies, elderly or disabled residents first.
